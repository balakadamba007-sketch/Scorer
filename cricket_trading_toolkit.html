Hard Rules

21 violations this season. Each rule costs £100–200 per breach. These are permanent and non-negotiable.

The 7 rules — never break
1
No PP trade before over 3
Zero PP line bets until 3 complete overs bowled. Broken 7× this season. Cost £700+. No exceptions for any reason.
2
One order per market per innings
One bet per market per innings. Never re-enter the same market in the same innings. Broken 7×. Cost £700+.
3
Second back — calculate greenbook first
Before any second back on same team: calculate combined position. If combined liability exceeds £500 = do not place. Broken 5×. Cost £500+.
4
No lay target = do not back
Write lay target odds, score trigger, and lay stake before every single back. No back placed without all three written. Broken 5×. Cost £500+.
5
Never back UNDER straight
Always back/lay structure on UNDER bets. Straight UNDER = no cash out = locked in. Broken 3×. Cost £300+.
6
15 Ov line — permanently banned
This market is banned forever. Never trade it again. Banned after 28 Mar.
7
Conviction game: hold to 1.45
If 3 YES signals (matchup + pitch + sim gap) = conviction. Lay at 1.45, not 1.75. Never greenbook a conviction game at 1.93.
